# V3 « Atelier »

- **Hero:** a live monogram on cobalt leather; visitors type up to three initials and switch the finish (marquage à chaud, gravure, embossage, feuille d'or). Light follows the pointer.
- **Hero → techniques:** the hero pins and the camera pulls back: the full-screen leather turns out to be a piece (trimmed to a 4:5 card on phones), its seam is sewn in gold as you scroll, and it lands among the eight signature techniques. Each tile links to its technique; the finish chosen in the hero is outlined in the ring.
- **Ground:** brand ink `#02374F` everywhere, deeper ink for alternating sections; gold `#D0AB7E` is the light.
- **Type:** Bodoni Moda (engraving capitals, display) with Geist (text). Neither V1's Cormorant/Inter nor V2's Jost.
- **Grid:** pinned two-column technique index, masonry of works with the rubric filter folded into the heading, dense square contact sheets on rubric pages.
- **Motion:** tactile: press, settle, catch the light (stamp on load, settle-in reveals, gold glint across the logo wall), no slides and no slow fades.
